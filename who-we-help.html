Why we split it this way

How customers find you decides everything else.

A plumber and a SaaS founder need almost nothing in common. One has thousands of people searching every month with immediate intent, and wins by being the first result with the most reviews. The other has nobody searching at all, and wins by building a category story and going to buyers directly.

Run the plumber's playbook at the startup and you'll optimize for keywords nobody types. Run the startup's playbook at the plumber and you'll write thought leadership while a competitor takes the calls.

So we start from how demand actually reaches you, then choose the channels that fit. The four groups below are simply the four patterns we see most.
