Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Who we are

Navren Agency ("Navren", "we", "us") is a marketing agency. You can reach us at hello@navrenagency.com for anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your information.

What we collect

Only what you type in. We don't ask for anything sensitive, and we don't want it.

  • Contact form: your name and email, plus optionally phone, company and website; what you tell us about your situation; and how you heard about us.
  • Questionnaire: your answers about your business, plus your name, email and website.
  • Email: whatever you send us, if you write directly.

We don't run advertising trackers or third-party analytics profiling on this site. Our host records standard server logs, as every web host does.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your inquiry and, if it goes further, to scope work
  • To prepare for a call so we're not asking you things you already told us
  • To keep our own records of who we've spoken to

That's the whole list. We don't use it for anything you didn't get in touch about.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information, ever, to anyone
  • We don't add you to a marketing list because you filled in a form. If we ever start a newsletter, you'd have to opt in
  • We don't share your details with other clients or partners without asking you first

Who else can see it

A small number of service providers process this information so the site and our email work. Today that means our website host and our email provider. They act on our instructions and can't use your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that don't become work are deleted within two years. If we work together, we keep records for as long as we need them for the engagement and for whatever period our accountant tells us is required after it ends.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@navrenagency.com and we'll deal with it within thirty days. We won't ask why.

We are a Washington State company and handle information under the laws that apply to us.

Cookies

This site doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. If that ever changes (for instance if we add analytics) this policy will be updated first and you'll be asked where consent is required.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. Material changes affecting information we already hold will be sent to you directly.
