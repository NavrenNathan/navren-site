Choosing

Two channels, run properly, beat five run thinly.

The temptation with a list like this is to want all of it. What actually moves a young company is picking the one or two channels your buyers already use, working them until they return reliably, then adding the next.

That's what the first month is for. We look at how customers find you now, where the demand already sits, and what a customer is worth, then recommend the order. You get the reasoning alongside the recommendation, so you can argue with the logic rather than just the conclusion.
