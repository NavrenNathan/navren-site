Communications

The right message, in front of the right people.

Whether you're building a pipeline, raising capital, or strengthening the relationships that carry the company, it comes down to reaching specific people with something worth their attention.

How it runs

Credibility first, then the ask.

A visible, credible presence is what makes the direct approach land, so it comes first.

First

Make you findable

Profile and posts, so that when someone checks you out (and they will) there's a person there with something to say. This alone lifts every reply rate that follows.

Then

Build the list

Who actually has this problem, by name. Small and accurate beats large and vague every time, and it's the difference between outreach and spam.

Then

Write the sequence

Short, specific, and obviously written for the person receiving it. Then measure replies rather than opens, because opens stopped meaning anything.

How it builds

Fewer messages, better aimed.

Cold outreach works when the message could only have been written to that one person. Specificity is what earns the reply, and it also protects your domain reputation, which volume quietly erodes.

So we research the list properly and write to it properly. It takes longer to set up, and it keeps working month after month.

Straight answers

What founders ask us about communications.

Isn't cold email spam?
It is when it's untargeted and automated at volume. There's a real difference between messaging fifty people who genuinely have the problem with something specific, and blasting five thousand. We only do the first, and we work within the rules that govern it, including CAN-SPAM.
I don't have time to post on LinkedIn.
That's what ghostwriting is for. We interview you occasionally, write from that, and you approve. Realistically under an hour of your time a week.
Will it still sound like me?
That's the whole test. We work from recordings of how you actually talk, and you approve every post. If it doesn't sound like you, it isn't working, for you or for the reader.
Do you do the sending and the tooling?
We'll set up the sequences and the tooling with you, but the sending happens from your domain and your accounts. That's deliberate, you own the relationship and the reputation, not us.
