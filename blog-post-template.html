The headline of the post.

A standfirst sentence or two that sets up what the piece argues, the same way the hero sub works on every other page.

Open with the point. The lede class gives the first paragraph the same weight it has on the rest of the site, so a post starts the way a section does.

Body copy sits at a comfortable reading measure. Write in the same voice used across the site: plain, specific, and willing to name the trade-off rather than talk around it.

A section heading

Headings use the same h2 treatment as every other page, so a post reads as part of the site rather than a separate template.

  • A list item, styled the way lists already are elsewhere
  • Another point worth pulling out of the paragraph
  • A third, if the argument needs it

Another section

Worth pulling out
