How we'd approach it

Where we'd start, and why.

For commerce, the goal is simple: get more value from the traffic and customers you already have, then scale what works.

First

Conversion and retention

Make more of the demand you already have. We tighten the customer journey, improve key conversion points, and build systems that turn first purchases into repeat revenue.

Then

Acquisition and channel strategy

Find the channels that can bring in customers efficiently. We test where demand is strongest, measure what converts, and focus spend where the economics make sense.

Alongside

Measurement and optimization

Track what is driving revenue and where customers are dropping off. We use that data to refine the message, improve performance, and keep resources focused on what is working.

Why this order

Get more from what you already have.

Every point of repeat rate you gain makes each acquisition dollar worth more, which is why it comes first when growth is the goal.

It also means we can show a return early, from traffic and customers you already have, without asking you to increase spend.

Straight answers

What store owners ask us.

How do we know which marketing channels are worth investing in?
We look at your margins, customer value, buying cycle, and where demand already exists. Then we test the channels most likely to work and focus spend where the economics make sense.
Should we focus on getting more traffic or converting more of what we already have?
Usually both matter, but not at the same time. If the site is leaking demand, buying more traffic just makes the leak more expensive. We identify the biggest constraint first and work from there.
How do we increase repeat purchases without constantly discounting?
Retention is more than sending more emails. We look at the full post-purchase experience, messaging, timing, offers, and customer journey to create more reasons for people to come back.
Can you help if we already have an agency running paid ads?
Yes. We can work around an existing team and focus on strategy, conversion, retention, search, content, or the parts of the customer journey that make paid acquisition perform better.
